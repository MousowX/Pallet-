ASTM D6251-style cleated panel walls: a 1×4 cleat frame (rails and edge cleats, plus intermediate cleats no farther apart than the max cleat spacing), plywood sheathing. Edge cleats interior keep the crate flush to the pallet footprint; exterior lap the side-to-end corner joint (stronger corner, more interior room) but stand one cleat thickness proud of the base on each face. Skirted: panels drop over the base edge and are nailed horizontally into it (fasteners in shear — standard for export). Side and end laps are set apart: keep the end lap at the deck so the forks fit between the stringers, and stop the side lap above the notches for side entry. On-deck: bottom rail lag-screwed down into stringers (detachable, weaker). Plywood is ISPM-15 exempt, and so are cleats ripped from it; the lumber base and lumber cleats need the HT stamp. Plywood sheets are counted by laying every part on the sheet with the saw cut between parts; cleat strips run along the face grain (sheet length).
