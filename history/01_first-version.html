ASTM D6251-style cleated panel walls: 1×4 frame (2 rails + verticals at edges and every ≤ spacing), plywood sheathing. Edge cleats interior keep the crate flush to the pallet footprint; exterior lap the side-to-end corner joint (stronger corner, more interior room) but stand ct proud of the base on each face. Skirted: panel + bottom rail drop over the base edge, nailed horizontally into deck edge and stringer ends (fasteners in shear — standard for export). On-deck: bottom rail lag-screwed down into stringers (detachable, weaker). Plywood is ISPM-15 exempt; lumber base and cleats are not.
